WEEK 1: SOLVING LINEAR SYSTEMS
LAEF 2E -- Chapter 1: Solving Linear Systems (Sections 1.1-1.8)
Application sections: Network Flows (graphs to linear systems); Structural Analysis (forces in trusses)

CORE CONCEPTS:

1. Linear Systems
   - Definition and matrix form Ax = b (Def 1.1)
   - Solution types: unique, none, infinitely many
   - Existence and obstruction: which right-hand sides are reachable (Ex 1.2)
   - Two geometric readings: intersecting hyperplanes (rows) vs. combinations of columns

2. Row Reduction
   - Elementary row operations R1, R2, R3 (Def 1.5)
   - Row echelon form (Def 1.6) and reduced row echelon form
   - Pivots, pivot columns, free variables
   - Row operations preserve the solution set (and the null space and row space)
   - General solution as particular + homogeneous

3. Rank
   - Rank as the number of pivots (Def 1.12)
   - The first numerical invariant attached to a matrix
   - What rank predicts about existence and uniqueness
   - Forward link: rank reappears as dim(image) in Chapter 3; the two must agree

4. Inverse & Invertibility
   - Nonsingularity as a list of equivalent conditions (Def 1.7)
   - Inverse by row reduction on [A | I]
   - Determinants are ASSUMED from multivariable calculus; used as a test, never as a
     computational tool -- pivots are the working criterion
   - Neumann series: (I - A)^{-1} = I + A + A^2 + ... when A^k -> 0 entrywise (Lemma 1.8)

5. Composition & Elimination
   - Row operations as left multiplication by elementary matrices
   - Elimination read as a factorization rather than a procedure

6. LU & PLU Decomposition
   - A = LU with L unit lower triangular, U upper triangular (Def 1.9)
   - L is assembled from the elimination multipliers (Ex 1.10)
   - PA = LU when row exchanges are required (Def 1.11)
   - Why factor at all: one factorization, many right-hand sides
   - Solve by forward substitution (Ly = b) then back substitution (Ux = y)

7. Practicalities of Linear Systems (1.8)
   - Conditioning: when a small perturbation of b produces a large change in x
   - Partial pivoting and numerical stability; the elimination growth factor
   - Cost: elimination is O(n^3), each subsequent solve is O(n^2)
   - The condition number is defined precisely in Chapter 10 via singular values;
     here it is a qualitative warning, not a formula

ESSENTIAL MATRIX TYPES (1.2):
- Identity and diagonal
- Elementary matrices
- Permutation matrices (Def 1.3)
- Upper and lower triangular
- Block and block-diagonal structure (Ex 1.4)
- Symmetric matrices

ESSENTIAL SKILLS (v2, 2026-09-10 -- conceptual: recognize, decide, distinguish, explain why):
- [W01.S01] Read rank, pivot columns, and the number of free variables off an echelon form, and
  know why rank is the number of pivots and not the number of rows
- [W01.S02] Classify a system as having no, one, or infinitely many solutions from rank and
  augmented-rank alone, without solving
- [W01.S03] Recognize the solution set as particular + homogeneous: an affine translate of the
  null space, and know its dimension
- [W01.S04] Recognize nonsingularity from any one of its equivalent conditions (pivot count,
  trivial null space, unique solutions, nonzero determinant) and move between them
- [W01.S05] Know what elementary matrices do: row operations as LEFT multiplication, and the
  order in which a sequence of them composes
- [W01.S06] Know what row operations preserve (solution set, row space, null space) and what
  they do not (column space)
- [W01.S07] Recognize an LU or PA = LU factorization and say what each factor records; know when
  the permutation is required
- [W01.S08] Explain WHY one factors: one elimination, many right-hand sides; forward then back
  substitution; the O(n³) vs O(n²) asymmetry
- [W01.S09] Recognize when permutation or block structure decouples a system
- [W01.S10] Say what conditioning warns about and what it does not — and that a small
  determinant is not the diagnostic

TYPICAL MCQ TRAPS:
- Reporting the number of rows (or nonzero entries) instead of the number of pivots as rank
- Confusing an inconsistent row (no solution) with a free variable (infinitely many)
- Sign errors on the multipliers stored in L
- Believing det(A) != 0 implies A is well-conditioned
- Assuming row operations preserve the column space (they preserve row space and null space)
- Multiplying out A = LU to "check" while ignoring the required permutation
